

## End to End ML Project

1. Look at the Big Picture
2. Get the data
3. Discover and Visualize the data to gain insights
4. Prepare data for ML 
5. Select a model and train it
6. Fine-tine your model
7. Present your solution
8. Launch, monitor and maintain


### Frame the Problem

1. What is the business objective?
2. How does the company expect to use and benefit from the model?
3. What does the current solution look like?

### Translate to ML Problem

1. Is it supervise, unsupervised or reinforcement learning?
2. Is it a classification task or regression task?
3. Select a performance measure to evalute your model
4. Check the assumptions
